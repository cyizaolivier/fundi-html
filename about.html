About FundiLink

FundiLink connects households across Rwanda with verified local artisans and repair professionals - electricians, plumbers, carpenters, tailors, and more - so booking trusted help is as easy as searching a trade and picking a fundi nearby.

Our Mission

To make it fast and reliable for anyone to find skilled local service providers, while giving fundis a simple way to reach more clients and manage their bookings.

Our Vision

To become the go-to platform for local services across East Africa, built by and for the communities it serves.
